# Privacy Policy - World of Ronodin

Last updated: 2026-10-25

World of Ronodin ("the game") is a hobby game made by one person. This page says what the game stores about you, why, and how to have it removed.
Contact: worldofronodin@gmail.com

## What the game stores

**On your device (always)**
- Your character save, your settings (volume, controls, display mode) and a few "seen" hints. They stay on your device and are not sent anywhere unless you sign in (see below).

**If you sign in with Google (optional, you can also play offline)**
- Google sign-in gives the game your Google account identifier, your email address and your profile name. The game uses the identifier to know which save is yours. The email address and profile name are not shown to other players.
- Your character save is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account identifier, so you can continue on another device. It holds your character name, level, skills, items, spells, timed boosts and in-game progress. Only you can read it.
- A **public build snapshot** is stored too, so other players can fight a "ghost" of your build: your character name, level, power, equipped gear and spells. It is readable by other signed-in players. It carries no email address.

**Weekend tournaments (only if you sign in)**
- When you start a tournament run, the game stores in Firebase that you started it (your account identifier, the tournament, the weekend and the server time), so the time of the run cannot be changed.
- At the end of a run the game **asks whether to publish your score**. Only if you say yes, your **character name, your main character's level and your scores of that weekend** (best damage in one fight and the XP of your skills) go on a public leaderboard that every signed-in player can read. If you say no, nothing is published.
- A private record of your own results per weekend is stored under your account identifier. Only you can read it.
- You can remove all of it with Reset Character (see below).

**Advertising (Google AdMob)**
- The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for a boost). Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/EEA and UK), the game first shows Google's consent form, and you can say no to personalised ads.
- If you buy the Supporter pack, ads are removed.

**Purchases (Google Play Billing)**
- Purchases are handled by Google Play. The game never sees your payment details, only whether a purchase was made.

The game does not use analytics or tracking of its own, and it does not sell your data.

## Why
- Google sign-in and Firebase: to keep your save safe and to let you continue on another device.
- Public snapshot: for the ghost fights against other players.
- Leaderboard: to show the scores of the weekend tournaments, only after you say yes.
- AdMob: to pay for the game.

## How long, and how to delete your data
- **In the game:** Options → **Reset Character**. This deletes your character on the device **and** your cloud save **and** your public build snapshot **and** your tournament scores and records. If you are offline at that moment, the deletion is done the next time you sign in.
- **By email:** write to worldofronodin@gmail.com from the address of your Google account and ask for your data to be deleted. It is deleted within 30 days.
- Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device. Cloud data stays until you reset the character or ask for deletion.

## Children
The game is not meant for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Other services
Google Firebase, Google Sign-In, Google AdMob and Google Play have their own privacy policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
