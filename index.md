# Privacy Policy - World of Ronodin

Last updated: 2026-11-01

World of Ronodin ("the game") is a hobby game made by one person. This page says what the game stores about you, why, and how to have it removed.
Contact: worldofronodin@gmail.com

## What the game stores

**On your device (always)**
- Your character save, your settings (volume, controls, display mode) and a few "seen" hints. They stay on your device and are not sent anywhere unless you sign in (see below).

**If you sign in with Google (optional, you can also play offline)**
- Google sign-in gives the game your Google account identifier, your email address and your profile name. The game uses the identifier to know which save is yours. The email address and profile name are not shown to other players.
- Your character save is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account identifier, so you can continue on another device. It holds your character name, level, skills, items, spells, timed boosts and in-game progress. Only you can read it.
- Your main character is **not shown to other players**: the game stores no public copy of it. (Older versions stored a public build snapshot; the game removes it from the cloud the first time you play after updating.) The only thing of it that other players can see is what you choose to show on the **main game boards** (next section).

**Main game boards (only if you sign in)**
- When you choose your game name, the game tells you that, **when you play signed in, your game name, level and totals are shown on the leaderboards**; this is on by default for a new character and you can turn it off at any time in Options ("Main game boards"). Older characters that answered the question keep their answer; the others are still asked once. If it is on, your **game name**, your main character's level and its totals (total damage dealt, hero XP and the XP of your skills) are stored in Firebase and can be read by other signed-in players. They are sent when your save is stored to the cloud, and only when a total grew. The game name is a name you chose yourself: it does not have to be your real name, and nothing on the boards is linked to your real name or email address. If it is off (or you were not asked yet), nothing is sent.
- The boards are kept per phase of the game (for example "Closed test", "Preseason"); a phase's boards stay readable after the next phase starts.
- Reset Character gives your entries on these boards a **made-up name** instead of your game name, like the tournament scores; to have them removed, write to worldofronodin@gmail.com.

**Weekend tournaments (only if you sign in)**
- When you start a tournament run, the game stores in Firebase that you started it (your account identifier, the tournament, the weekend and the server time), so a run cannot be stretched.
- The same choice covers the tournament boards: it is on by default for a new character (see above) and you can change it at any time in Options ("Tournament scores"). Older characters that did not answer yet are asked after their first run. If it is on, your **game name**, your main character's level and your scores of the weekend can be read by other signed-in players. The game name is a name you chose yourself: it does not have to be your real name, and other players may use the same name. If it is off, only you can see your results.
- A private record of your own results per weekend is stored under your account identifier. Only you can read it.
- During a tournament run the game also stores a **public ghost of your run character** for that weekend, so other runs can meet it as an opponent: its level, power, equipped gear and spells, under a **made-up name** (for example "Cinderfang"), never your game name and never your main character. Starting a run is the agreement to this: the notice before the run says so. It does not depend on your leaderboard choice and carries no email address. It is readable by other signed-in players.
- Reset Character removes your private records, your run, the ghost of your run character and your save. **Scores you chose to show stay on the leaderboards, but under a made-up name** (for example "Brass Fox 42") instead of your game name, so the ranks of other players do not change. They still carry the same internal account identifier as before; nothing else about you is kept with them. To have even those scores removed, write to worldofronodin@gmail.com.

**Achievements, Obols and inviting friends (only if you sign in)**
- Your achievements (counters such as monsters defeated, the achievements you reached) and your Obols (the in-game coin) are part of your character save. Only you can read them.
- **Inviting a friend:** the game gives you a personal invite code made from your account identifier and stores it in Firebase so a friend can use it. If you type a friend's email address to invite them, it is only used to open your own mail app with a ready message: **the game does not send the email and does not store or collect that address.**
- If you enter an invite code yourself, the game stores in Firebase who invited you (the account identifier of the inviter) and your character level, so the inviter's game can count friends who reached a level. The inviter sees a number only, never your name or email address. Nobody can read these records except the two accounts involved.
- Reset Character removes your invite code, the record of who invited you and your entry in that person's friend count.

**Update check (on a phone, no sign-in needed)**
- When the game starts on a phone it reads one small public file (`latest.json`, the number of the newest version) from this site's host, GitHub Pages, to see whether a newer version is available. The game sends no account, save or device data with it. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual request data, and the game does not keep the answer.

**Advertising (Google AdMob)**
- The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for a boost). Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/EEA and UK), the game first shows Google's consent form, and you can say no to personalised ads.
- If you buy the Supporter pack, ads are removed.

**Purchases (Google Play Billing)**
- Purchases are handled by Google Play. The game never sees your payment details, only whether a purchase was made.
- For each purchase the game keeps a **purchase record**: the order number and purchase token Google Play gives, the product, the price shown and the date. It is kept on your device and, if you sign in, in your cloud save (only you can read it), and you can see it in the Shop ("My purchases"). It is your proof of purchase: it lets the game give back what you bought (also after Reset Character or on a new device) and lets us check a purchase if something goes wrong.

The game does not use analytics or tracking of its own, and it does not sell your data.

## Why
- Google sign-in and Firebase: to keep your save safe and to let you continue on another device.
- Run ghost: for the ghost fights against other tournament runs.
- Invite code and friend count: to give an achievement for bringing friends to the game.
- Leaderboards: to show the scores of the weekend tournaments and the totals of the main game boards, only if you say yes.
- AdMob: to pay for the game.

## How long, and how to delete your data
- **In the game:** Options → **Reset Character**. This deletes your character on the device **and** your cloud save **and** any public build snapshot of an older version **and** the ghost of your tournament run **and** your private tournament records; the scores you chose to show stay under a made-up name (see above). If you are offline at that moment, the deletion is done the next time you sign in. Your **purchase records** are kept, so you keep what you bought with real money; the new character's cloud save holds them again. To have them removed too, write to us (below).
- **By email:** write to worldofronodin@gmail.com from the address of your Google account and ask for your data to be deleted. It is deleted within 30 days.
- Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device. Cloud data stays until you reset the character or ask for deletion.

## Children
The game is not meant for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect data from them.

## Other services
Google Firebase, Google Sign-In, Google AdMob and Google Play have their own privacy policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy
GitHub Pages (the update check and this page) has its own: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

## Korte samenvatting (Nederlands)
- Zonder inloggen blijft alles op je toestel.
- Tournooi-scores en hoofdspel-borden (gamenaam, level, totale schade, skill-XP, scores; geen echte naam of e-mailadres) zijn zichtbaar voor andere spelers wanneer je ingelogd speelt. Dat staat standaard aan voor een nieuw karakter: het spel zegt het op het scherm waar je je gamenaam kiest, en je zet het altijd uit in Options. Oudere karakters die al antwoordden houden hun antwoord. Staat het uit, dan wordt er niets verstuurd. Na Reset Character staan de scores onder een verzonnen naam.
- Met Google-login staat je savegame in Firebase (alleen jij kan die lezen). Je hoofdkarakter wordt niet aan andere spelers getoond. Alleen als je een toernooi-run start, staat er een publieke ghost van je run-karakter (level, kracht, uitrusting, spells, onder een verzonnen naam, nooit je gamenaam); die start is je toestemming. Je e-mailadres wordt niet getoond aan anderen.
- Uitnodigen: je e-mailadres-invoer voor een vriend opent alleen je eigen mail-app; het spel verstuurt of bewaart dat adres niet. Een uitnodigingscode en wie je uitnodigde (alleen account-id's en een level) staan in Firebase om vrienden te tellen.
- Op een telefoon leest het spel bij het opstarten één klein openbaar bestand (het nummer van de nieuwste versie) van GitHub Pages om een update voor te stellen; er worden geen gegevens van jou meegestuurd (GitHub ziet wel je IP-adres, zoals elke website).
- Optionele advertenties via Google AdMob (met toestemmingsformulier waar dat verplicht is); aankopen via Google Play. Per aankoop bewaart het spel een aankoopbewijs (ordernummer en token van Google Play, product, prijs, datum) op je toestel en in je cloud-save; je ziet het in de Shop ("My purchases"). Reset Character houdt het bij, zodat je behoudt wat je met echt geld kocht.
- Alles verwijderen: Options → Reset Character (wist ook de cloud-save en de ghost; scores die je zichtbaar maakte blijven staan onder een verzonnen naam, en aankoopbewijzen blijven bewaard), of mail naar worldofronodin@gmail.com om ook die te laten wissen.
